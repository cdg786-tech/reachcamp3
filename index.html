The Engagement

Three phases. Ninety days.

Borrowed from how an expedition moves: read the route, do the time on trail, acclimate to the new altitude.
I
Weeks 1–3  ·  What we find

Route Planning

We pull three years of project records, timesheets, fees, and signed contracts. The result is a written read of the firm: where margin is leaking, where pursuit is misfiring, where the systems carrying the business are quietly improvising. Quick wins flagged for the first thirty days.

II
Weeks 4–9  ·  What we build

Time on Trail

We install the tracking, fee models, dashboards, and pursuit tools the firm needs, inside the software your team already uses. Nothing new is licensed. Nothing new is bought. Each tool passes the minutes-a-week test before it ships.

III
Weeks 10–12  ·  What you keep

Acclimatization

The system goes live. We hold cadence with the principals through the first month of real use, calibrating against real data, then step away. The firm runs on systems your team operates in minutes a week, with a written operating manual and pursuit criteria set for the quarter ahead.
